  • Answer Not at all to every mood and worry question for a green result.
  • Answer Several days to PHQ-9 item 9 (thoughts of being better off dead) to see the safety message and the same-day call-back.
  • Choose Never to the first alcohol question to see the skip rule.
  • Answer that a lender is holding your ID card to read the money and ID advice.

Anonymous wellbeing survey

This is the full research survey exactly as a student sees it, on a laptop or a phone. A student gets an automatic anonymous code, reads the protections, agrees, and answers only the questions that apply. Share the link below, or print the QR code.

Expert input

A short review for the five-staff expert group (for example a nurse, a medical officer, a chaplain, a welfare officer and a lecturer). Each person rates every design statement from 1 (not important) to 9 (essential) and adds suggestions. No names are asked. No meeting is needed.
